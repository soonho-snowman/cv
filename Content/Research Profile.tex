Soonho is a Human–Computer Interaction (HCI) and design researcher. His current research interests include: (1) \textit{Korean LGBTQ+ communities}, where he collaborates with community organizations to understand how queer individuals endure and navigate hostile socio-technical conditions, while co-envisioning supportive technologies;  (2) \textit{Spiritual AI}, where he critically designs and envisions human–AI interactions beyond efficiency and accuracy; (3) \textit{Death and technology}, where he investigates non-normative experiences of death and designs for digital legacy and end-of-life preparation, particularly for marginalized populations; and (4) \textit{Tech- and data-driven governance}, where he examines how people engage with data infrastructures and policies to maintain and protect their ways of being. Grounded in critical, feminist, design, and queer approaches, his work has been presented at venues such as ACM CHI, CSCW, and DIS, and recognized at Prix Ars Electronica.
