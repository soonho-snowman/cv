Soonho is a Human–Computer Interaction (HCI) and design researcher interested in how technology can support \textit{contentment} amid the realities of everyday life. Rather than treating technology as a means of solving problems to achieve an ideal, he explores how people can find meaningful ways of living within conditions that are difficult to change. Grounded in critical, feminist, queer, and design approaches, his work spans Korean LGBTQ+ communities, spiritual AI, death and digital legacy, and technology-driven governance. His work has been presented at ACM CHI, CSCW, and DIS, and recognized by Prix Ars Electronica.
